## Generated visuals

Six new text-free category hero illustrations were generated with imagegen and saved as optimized local WebP files in `assets/images/categories/`:

- `transfer.webp`: Moroccan female agency employee, smartphone and globe; illustrative person.
- `telecom.webp`: smartphone, connectivity and blank receipt elements.
- `insurance.webp`: protective shield, vehicles, family and education/travel symbolism.
- `administration.webp`: hands completing blank documents at an office desk.
- `gaming.webp`: controller, gaming phone and electric-blue lighting.
- `travel.webp`: plane, luggage, globe and travel documents.

Two previously generated local illustrations are retained for CNSS/AMO and finance, under both `assets/images/` (homepage) and `assets/images/categories/` (service hero/detail): blank administrative/healthcare forms, and a financial consultation still life. All generated files exist in the project. Source generation file paths are recorded in `assets/generated-sources.json` for local provenance.

## Vector assets

37 original consistent SVG pictograms are generated by `tools/build.cjs` into `assets/icons/`. They share the same line weights, navy/royal-blue strokes and selected gold accents. Service and benefit icons are vectors, not emojis. `favicon.svg` is a simple generic D monogram, not a recreation of an official trademark.

## Practical limitations

Extracted artwork has the resolution of the supplied references and will soften on wide screens. A real authenticated agency photograph cannot be generated: none was provided. About and Contact therefore use the clearly captioned supplied storefront artwork. No generated scene is presented as a real agency location, team member, official document or company logo.
