# Brand vector sources

These local SVG files were downloaded from the [Simple Icons project](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), through its documented pinned `simple-icons@16` jsDelivr package:

`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16/icons/{slug}.svg`

Downloaded slugs: `playstation`, `steam`, `roblox`, `pubg`, `fortnite`, `emirates`, `qatarairways`, `westernunion`, `moneygram`.

The `whatsapp` SVG is also used for the standard WhatsApp action, replacing the original approximate UI drawing.

The archived `simple-icons@12/icons/xbox.svg` and `simple-icons@13/icons/nintendo.svg` are used for Xbox and Nintendo brand identification. Their versioned paths are retained for reproducibility.

Files are used to identify services that customers may enquire about, without asserting affiliation or an authorized partnership. Original supplied reference logos are retained for brands without a verified vector file.
